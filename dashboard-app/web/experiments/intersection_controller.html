State logic

The highlighted step is the current safe crossing state inferred from the active lamps. You can also click a state card to force that mode and watch the cars wait or move.

MAIN_GREEN
Main street green, side street red, pedestrian lamp off.
MAIN_YELLOW
Main street yellow, side street red, pedestrian lamp off.
SIDE_GREEN
Side street green, main street red, pedestrian lamp off.
WALK
Both roads red and walk lamp on for the pedestrian crossing.
